# ☕ Bright Coffee Shop – Sales Analysis

## 📌 1. Project Overview

The Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis project focuses on analysing coffee shop sales data for the period **1 January 2023 to 30 June 2023**. The purpose of the project is to understand business performance, explore customer purchasing patterns, identify product trends, and evaluate revenue across different store locations.

The dataset contains approximately 61,000 records and provides information about transaction dates, sales, units sold, product categories, product types, revenue, and store locations.

The analysis covers three store locations:
- Astoria
- Hell's Kitchen
- Lower Manhattan

The project aims to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data preparation, analysis, and interactive visualizations.

## ⚙️ 2. Project Process

### 1. Data Understanding and Preparation
Used Microsoft Excel to explore the dataset, understand its structure, review the available fields, and prepare the information for analysis.

### 2. Data Exploration and Analysis
Explored the dataset to understand sales performance and identify trends across different business areas, including:
- Monthly and quarterly revenue performance.
- Product categories and individual product performance.
- Sales across different store locations.
- Weekday and weekend sales patterns.
- Morning, afternoon, and evening sales activity.
- Units sold and transaction performance.

### 3. Data Processing
Used Databricks as part of the project's data analytics toolkit to explore data processing and analytical workflows.

### 4. Dashboard Development
Developed interactive dashboards using Power BI and Looker Studio to communicate the findings. The dashboards incorporate visualizations, key performance indicators (KPIs), and interactive elements to make the information easier to explore and understand.

### 5. Planning and UX Design
Used Miro for project planning and organizing ideas, Lovable for dashboard interface prototyping, and Canva for creating visual elements and presentation materials. The design approach focused on clear layouts, effective data presentation, and user-friendly navigation.

### 6. Business Insights
Reviewed the analysis to identify important sales patterns, understand product performance, compare store locations, and highlight potential opportunities for improving business performance.

## 📊 4. Project Summary

The Bright Coffee Shop project demonstrates how data analytics and business intelligence can be applied to understand sales performance and support business decision-making.

The analysis covers revenue trends, product performance, store comparisons, and purchasing patterns across a six-month period. Through interactive dashboards and visual reporting, the project makes it easier to identify trends, compare performance, and communicate important findings.

It also demonstrates the combination of technical data analysis and UX design principles to create dashboards that are informative, visually appealing, and easy to navigate.

The overall goal is to turn sales data into actionable business insights that can help stakeholders better understand performance and identify opportunities for business growth.
